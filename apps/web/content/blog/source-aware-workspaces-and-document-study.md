Learning rarely arrives in one format. Abstruck brings videos, PDFs, and PowerPoint slides into one [study workspace](/features). Ask about a source, open a citation to check the page or timestamp, and use your notes to practise.

## One workspace, different sources

Uploaded audio and videos have a player and a timestamped transcript. Select a timestamp to jump to that part of the recording.

For PDFs and PowerPoint files, Abstruck extracts text so the study tools can cite passages. Citations can take you to the relevant page or slide when the source provides that information.

## Keep the source in view

Read a passage, ask a question, and return to the original before making a note or generating flashcards and quizzes. On a phone, open the source in the full-width viewer to read it.

## Check the page or timestamp

- For audio and video, follow a citation back to the corresponding transcript timestamp.
- For documents, follow a citation to the relevant page or extracted passage.

Check the source before relying on an answer. A quote or timestamp helps you find the evidence and decide whether it supports the answer.

## Private by default

Uploaded sources are private. If you share a saved study set, choose which sources to include. Anyone with the link can read what you shared.

Once you’ve checked the source, try [active recall](/blog/active-recall-study-method) or [turn your notes into flashcards](/blog/turn-notes-into-flashcards-with-ai).
